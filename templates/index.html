      Why this is a Circular Doubly Linked List

      Why Circular? The final track points back to the first track, so playback naturally loops.

      Why Doubly Linked? Each track stores both prev and next references for direct backward and forward navigation.

      Complexity: Next and Previous are O(1), while finding a track by ID is O(n).
